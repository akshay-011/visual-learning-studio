# Cinematic Documentary Guide

The Cinematic Documentary template presents computer science and software systems as grand human and technological narratives.

### Key Rules
- Narration tone must be reflective, authoritative, and evocative.
- Visuals emphasize scale: global networks, pulsating fiber optic trunks, and microsecond packet journeys.
- Music selection should favor atmospheric, evolving beds with subtle orchestral or electronic pulses.
